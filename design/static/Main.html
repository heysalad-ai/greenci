User lifecycle

From the first red check to the hundredth green one

Seven stages, what people do in each, what GreenCI puts in front of them, and the one moment each stage has to land. The key's face carries the mood from stage to stage.

01 · Week −2

Discover

They

Spot a “Fixed with GreenCI” footer on a PR, a 20-second clip, or a sticker on a laptop.

Touchpoints
PR footerLandingSocial clipStickers
They feel

Curious. A bit sceptical of “AI fixes your build”.

Land this

A real red-to-green run, start to finish, in under 20 seconds of video.

02 · Week −1

Order

They

Compare plans, check repo support, order a deck, wait for it.

Touchpoints
PricingCheckoutOrder emailShipping
They feel

Hopeful. “Is this a gimmick on my desk?”

Land this

Connect GitHub before the deck arrives, so it's ready on the first press.

03 · Day 0

Unbox & pair

They

Open the box, plug in USB-C, press any key, pick repos.

Touchpoints
BoxDeckPairing flowCLI install
They feel

Delight, then impatience: “now what?”

Land this

Paired and showing “ready” within 2 minutes of opening the box.

04 · Minute 5

First fix

They

Run the practice repo's planted failure: Diagnose, Repair, Verify, merge.

Touchpoints
KeysRun viewFix PRFirst-fix email
They feel

“Oh. That actually worked.”

The aha moment

Don't wait for a real failure. The practice repo gives everyone a green run on day one.

05 · Weeks 1–4

Daily loop

They

Glance at the deck, approve from their phone, review fix PRs like a teammate's.

Touchpoints
NotificationsMobileCLIWeekly digest
They feel

Trust building, wary of noise.

Land this

Interrupt only for Failing and Needs you. Everything else waits for the digest.

06 · Month 2+

Team

They

Invite teammates, set which paths need a human, add decks for the team.

Touchpoints
InvitesRepo rulesTeam digestBilling
They feel

In control. Need to justify it to a lead.

Land this

A monthly report with real numbers: checks fixed, time red, PRs merged.

07 · Year 1

Renew & share

They

Renew, hit milestones, leave a review, gift a deck to a friend's team.

Touchpoints
MilestonesReview promptReferralSwag drop
They feel

Proud of a green streak. Happy to show it off.

Land this

At 100 merged fixes, a limited emerald keycap ships to them, free.

How it feels

Planned emotional curve. The dip is the wait for a first real failure, which the practice repo removes.
The 2am page

Phone shows the cause before they open a laptop. One tap: Repair.

The honest miss

When a fix doesn't verify, say so, show what was tried, hand it back.

The merge

Keys flash green once. The only celebration in the product.

The quiet week

Nothing failed. The digest says so in one line and doesn't invent drama.
